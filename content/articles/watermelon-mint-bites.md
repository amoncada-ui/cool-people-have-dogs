<p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> 3 ingredients, 5 steps, and most of the work is hands-off. Here's exactly how to make it.</p>
<h2 id="ingredients" style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 36px 0 14px;">Ingredients</h2>
<ul style="margin: 14px 0; padding-left: 22px; line-height: 1.9;">
<li>2 cups seedless watermelon, diced</li>
<li>1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped</li>
<li>1/4 cup plain coconut water or water</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="instructions" style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 36px 0 14px;">Instructions</h2>
<ol style="margin: 14px 0; padding-left: 22px; line-height: 1.9;">
<li>Add the diced watermelon, chopped mint, and coconut water to a blender.</li>
<li>Blend until smooth.</li>
<li>Pour the mixture into a bone-shaped or any silicone mold.</li>
<li>Freeze for at least 3 hours, or until solid.</li>
<li>Pop out and serve straight from the freezer on a hot day.</li>
</ol>
<div style="background: #FBF3EC; border: 1px solid #E8C4A8; border-radius: 10px; padding: 16px 20px; margin: 24px 0; font-size: 15px; line-height: 1.6;">
<strong style="color: #8A4A2E;">Good to know:</strong> Double-check that the watermelon is completely seedless — watermelon seeds and rind can both cause digestive upset.
        </div>
<h2 id="faq" style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 36px 0 14px;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p><strong>Is mint safe for dogs?</strong><br/>Yes, in small amounts — fresh mint (not the essential oil form) is generally safe and can even help freshen breath.</p>
<p><strong>How long do these keep frozen?</strong><br/>About a month in an airtight container or freezer bag.</p>
