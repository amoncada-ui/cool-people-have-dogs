<h1 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 700; font-size: 44px; line-height: 1.15; letter-spacing: -0.01em; margin: 18px 0 0; text-align: center;">Privacy Policy</h1>
<p style="font-size: 15px; color: #8A8071; margin: 16px 0 0; text-align: center;">Last updated September 28, 2026</p>

<article style="font-size: 17px; line-height: 1.75; color: #322D25; margin-top: 40px;">

<p>Cool People Have Dogs ("we," "us," "this site") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what choices you have. It applies to coolpeoplehavedogs.com and covers everyone who visits, whatever state or country you're visiting from.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Information we collect</h2>
<p><strong>Information you give us directly.</strong> If you sign up for our newsletter or request a free download (like a printable), we collect your email address and use it to send you what you asked for.</p>
<p><strong>Information collected automatically.</strong> Like most websites, our hosting and any analytics tools we use may automatically log standard technical data — things like your browser type, device, approximate location, and which pages you viewed — to help us understand how the site is used and to keep it running securely.</p>
<p><strong>Cookies.</strong> Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser. We use them (and allow certain partners to use them) for a few purposes:</p>
<ul style="padding-left: 22px; margin: 0 0 16px;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">Basic site functionality (remembering that you closed this notice, for example)</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">Understanding which articles are useful, if we have analytics enabled</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">Affiliate tracking — when you click a link to a retailer like Amazon, a cookie tells that retailer the referral came from us, which is how we earn a commission if you buy something</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">Embedded content like the Pinterest "Save" button, which Pinterest may use to recognize you if you're logged into Pinterest elsewhere</li>
</ul>
<p>We don't currently run display advertising, but if that changes, this policy will be updated to name those ad partners specifically. By continuing to use this site, you're agreeing to our use of cookies as described here. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings if you'd rather not allow them — doing so may affect how some parts of the site work, but the articles themselves will still load fine.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">How we use your information</h2>
<p>We use the information above to send the newsletter or download you signed up for, understand what content is worth making more of, keep the site secure, and — through affiliate cookies — get credit for referrals when you choose to buy something we've linked to. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Third parties we work with</h2>
<p>Depending on what's active at any given time, this can include: an email service provider (to send newsletters and deliver downloads), affiliate programs such as Amazon Associates (to track referral purchases), and Pinterest (through the save button embedded on articles). Each of these has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data once it reaches them.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Children's privacy</h2>
<p>This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Your rights</h2>
<p>If you're a California resident, you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to request its deletion; since we don't sell personal information, there's no "opt-out of sale" needed. If you're in the EU or UK, you have similar rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent for cookies at any time. To exercise any of these rights, just email us — see below.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Data security</h2>
<p>We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We may update this page as the site grows — for example, if we add analytics or advertising. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">Contact us</h2>
<p>Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out any time at <a class="cphd-inlinelink" href="mailto:hello@coolpeoplehavedogs.com">hello@coolpeoplehavedogs.com</a>.</p>
