<article style="font-size: 17px; line-height: 1.75; color: #322D25; margin-top: 48px;">
<p>I started this site the way most people start these things — I googled a dumb question about my own dog at 11pm and got seven contradictory answers, three of which were clearly written by nobody who'd ever owned a dog. So I started writing the site I wished I'd found: real answers, written by someone who actually lives with a dog, checked against sources I'd trust myself.</p>
<p>I've had a dog in my house for most of my adult life and I'm based in San Antonio, where "is it too hot to walk him right now" is a genuinely useful question about eight months of the year. That's part of why this site leans practical — the advice here is filtered through what actually works in a real house with a real dog in it, not just what sounds right in theory.</p>
<h2 id="editorial-standards" style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">How we research and fact-check</h2>
<p>I'm not a veterinarian, and I'm not going to pretend to be one. What I do instead: every article gets checked against primary sources — organizations like the <a class="cphd-inlinelink" href="https://www.akc.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">AKC</a>, <a class="cphd-inlinelink" href="https://www.aspca.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">ASPCA</a>, <a class="cphd-inlinelink" href="https://www.avma.org/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">AVMA</a>, and <a class="cphd-inlinelink" href="https://www.vet.cornell.edu/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Cornell's College of Veterinary Medicine</a> — and cited when the claim matters. Our small editorial team reviews every piece before it goes live for accuracy and clarity, and anything that touches on a real health or safety question gets a second look before publishing. When something is genuinely urgent, we say so and point you to your vet or an emergency line, instead of guessing.</p>
<div style="background: #F3EEE2; border-left: 4px solid #C1622D; border-radius: 4px; padding: 18px 22px; margin: 24px 0;">
<div style="font-size: 12px; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: 0.06em; color: #C1622D; margin-bottom: 6px;">OUR RULE OF THUMB</div>
<p style="margin: 0; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.6;">If it's a "will this hurt my dog" question, we cite a source. If it's a "what should I name my dog" question, we just have opinions — clearly labeled as opinions.</p>

<h2 style="font-family: 'Fredoka', sans-serif; font-weight: 600; font-size: 26px; margin: 40px 0 14px;">How we make money</h2>
<p id="disclosure">Being upfront about this felt important: some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy something through them — at no extra cost to you. We only recommend things we'd actually give our own dogs, and a product being linked here is never why an article got written in the first place.</p>
